Biography

I am a Biomedical Engineering student at Islamic Azad University, Qazvin Branch, following the Bioelectric track. I am interested in how engineering can help people recover and maintain physical function, especially through technology designed around human needs.

A biosignal is most valuable when it can be turned into a useful response for the person, not only measured.

For the past two years I have worked as a research assistant at the Mechatronics Research Laboratory (MRL), mainly on mechatronic and electronic systems, including the design and experimental evaluation of aerial and mobile robotic platforms. In one of my projects I built a simulated closed-loop brain–computer interface with functional electrical stimulation (BCI-FES) for upper-limb stroke rehabilitation, in which EEG signals detect movement intent and trigger simulated stimulation.

I also research deep learning for dermatological image analysis, with a focus on melanoma, and I have served as a teaching assistant for Statics and Strength of Materials, where I adapted the material to biomedical examples such as the mechanics of human limbs. In the long term, I would like to pursue research in rehabilitation engineering and intelligent healthcare systems.
