What drives my work

A signal is most valuable when it becomes a useful response.

This idea guided my simulated closed-loop BCI-FES project for upper-limb stroke rehabilitation.

1

Sense

EEG signals are recorded while a person intends to move.

2

Decode

The system detects movement intent from the signal.

3

Stimulate

Detected intent triggers simulated functional electrical stimulation.

4

Respond

The limb response closes the loop and supports rehabilitation.

closed loop

Simulation-based project; the work is research and not a clinical system.
